Phone camera mapping

How it works

  1. Make sure your phone is on the same Wi-Fi/network as this PC.
  2. Scan the QR code. Your browser will warn about the certificate (it is generated locally by Cuttlefish) — accept it to continue.
  3. Lean the phone against something facing your case, tap Start camera, then Map LEDs.
  4. Cuttlefish flashes binary patterns on every LED. Each camera pixel decodes which LED it sees, so every LED is located in a few seconds — then devices are placed, scaled and rotated on the canvas automatically.

Tip: open the side panel or film through the glass at a slight angle to avoid reflections. Run it again from another side and the newest detections win.
